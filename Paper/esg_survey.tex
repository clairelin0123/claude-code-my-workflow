% --- Abstract ---
\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This paper presents a comprehensive review of the rapidly expanding literature
on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) issues. We highlight recent
advances in both theoretical modeling and empirical analysis. We first review
major theoretical ESG models. Following this, we survey empirical research in
six key areas: (1)~climate risk and insurance, (2)~regulation and policy,
(3)~ESG effects on investment, consumption, and employment,
(4)~ESG disclosure and assurance, (5)~the asset management industry, and
(6)~firm valuation, profitability, cost of capital, and asset pricing.
While significant progress has been made in understanding the impact of ESG
factors on firm behavior and market outcomes, we identify important gaps and
recommend future research directions.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Keywords:} ESG, sustainable finance, corporate governance, climate risk,
disclosure, asset pricing, cost of capital

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{JEL Codes:} G12, G32, G34, M14, Q54
\end{abstract}
